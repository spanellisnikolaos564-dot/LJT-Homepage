I am **Junteng Liu**, a first-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the **HKUST NLP Group**, The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by **Professor Junxian He**, who also advised me during my undergraduate studies at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU). I graduated from SJTU in June 2024.

My research focuses on **natural language processing (NLP)** and **machine learning**.

Research Interests and Skills
======
- Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Machine Learning
- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
- LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability

Education
